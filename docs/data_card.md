## Known limitations

- Name matching across collections fails for homonyms and generic seed names. Examples seen during collection: the seed 'Lotus Temple' (Delhi) matched the Golden Lotus tank in Madurai; 'Khyal' (a music form) matched a modern poem; 'Gopala' (a Pala king) matched an editor named Gopala Bhatta; 'Francis Xavier' matched a nun named after him. Dynasty seeds require a polity word next to the name, which removed matches such as 'Nanda' for Krishna's foster-father.
- Devanagari text tagged Hindi by the Internet Archive may be Sanskrit; script alone cannot tell them apart.
- Wellcome contributes pictures only: its Indian manuscripts are catalogued under Sanskrit titles, which seed-name searches do not reach.
- Internet Archive items with no year in their date or title are left out, because their public-domain mark cannot be checked; items dated 1950 or later are left out too.
- The neighbour selection's drift sample (`results/probes/neighbour_select_v1.md`) awaits the author's hand check; hand-scan decisions made during collection are marked 'pending author review' in `data/seeds/curated/`.

## Disclosure

Documents are collected, not generated: no synthetic or augmented text. Museum and library records keep their own fields as text; Wikipedia text comes from the TextExtracts API with revision IDs recorded (decision 0003).
